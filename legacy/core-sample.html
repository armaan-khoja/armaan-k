Marginalia

Mac app · Swift, SwiftUI

Claude reads photos of my pages and marks what's worth keeping: highlights, stars and margin notes. Marginalia turns its reply into a pass I can follow with a pen in hand. One step fills the screen, I mark it in the book, and the next one appears.

  • Three orders: page by page, highlights first, or one color at a time.
  • Move on with a key, a click, a tap or a two-finger swipe.
Walden, sample pass Step 1 of 5

Click or tap the page to mark a step, or press Space. The left arrow key goes back.

WPS Clubs

A clubs directory for WPS. Students sign in with their school email, join clubs and see what meets this week. In September I rebuilt the look: a photo mosaic sized by membership, and a week board up top.

Atlas

GitHub Action and web app · TypeScript

Free code explanations for GitHub that run on your own API key. Every pull request gets a plain-English summary and inline notes on the lines that need them. The web app explores any repo in your browser, and your key never leaves it.

  • Works with Claude or Gemini.
  • 186 tests across the Action, the core and the web app.
